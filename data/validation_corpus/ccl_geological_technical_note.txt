CENTRAL COALFIELDS LIMITED - GEOLOGICAL EXPLORATION NOTE
NORTH KARANPURA COALFIELD - TECHNICAL ASSESSMENT

1.0 Geological Summary
Detailed exploration in North Karanpura has proved significant coal resources in Barakar Formation.
The gross geological coal reserves estimated for North Karanpura basin are 86.0 MT.
Ash content varies between 28% and 34%, categorized predominantly under Grade G-11 and G-12.

2.0 Seam Sequence
Seam VII and Seam VIII exhibit continuous lateral extent across 14 km strike length.
